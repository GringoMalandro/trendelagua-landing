The Network

Tren del Agua is designed as an independent, EVM-compatible blockchain for public transfers and smart-contract applications. Network validation is governed through an authorized validator model, while users can hold and transfer AGUA without becoming validators.

AGUA: Native Currency

AGUA is designed to power transfers, network transaction fees, and digital services throughout the Tren del Agua ecosystem.
